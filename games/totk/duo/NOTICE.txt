Eden Duo companion host (libduo_host.so) -- Breath of the Wild and Tears of the Kingdom builds
==============================================================================================

The Breath of the Wild build and the Tears of the Kingdom build of this app each contain libduo_host.so and, under
assets/duo/, that game's companion package. They come from the Eden Duo project:

  Eden Duo (the dual-screen companion runtime, src/core/mods and what it needs)

      GPL-3.0-or-later (some files GPL-2.0-or-later)

  Eden Duo Companions (the title modules and their packages)
      https://github.com/P1R4N351/eden-duo-companions  branch totk-companion
                                                      commit 7e3faa6e1e17d246bde5c64f4c877b28e7bb87a9
      (a fork of https://github.com/igawa6/eden-duo-companions; that commit is upstream's
      2169928d2f301aa7bb3b13777ca8a94d0e73961d plus the Tears of the Kingdom companion)
      GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later per file)
        Breath of the Wild:     native/modules/01007EF00011E000.cpp, packages/BreathOfTheWild
        Tears of the Kingdom:   native/modules/0100F2C0115B6000.cpp with totk_romfs, wonder_assets and wonder_font,
                                packages/TearsOfTheKingdom. Its game-state reader, native/modules/totk_reader, is this
                                app's own MIT reader (games/totk/runtime/companion_snapshot.c and its headers, copied

Each build's libduo_host.so links one of the two modules (the game's own). It is a work based on the above and is
licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or (at your option) any later version; the licence text
follows. Because the APK includes this library, the Breath of the Wild APK and the Tears of the Kingdom APK are each,
as a whole, distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. The port's own source files (the runtime, the graphics layer, the app,
and duo/duo_port.h and duo/duo_state.h, the C interface the library is reached through) remain under the MIT licence
of this project (TotK-Native.txt), which permits that combination. Third-party code compiled into the library besides
Eden Duo (among it zstd's decompressor, BSD) is listed with its licence in Eden-Duo-third-party.txt.

The companion packages ship no game art: their images are drawn by code (ASSETS.provenance.json declares each), and
the Tears of the Kingdom package's text files only name files of the game. Game icons, the map, the fonts and all text
are read at run time from the player's own game files.

Source. The complete corresponding source of this APK is the whole source tree of this project at the commit named in
assets/build.txt (shown in the app's About screen) -- all of it, not only the directory games/totk/duo: libduo_host.so
also compiles games/totk/runtime/companion_state.h, and it is built by games/totk/android/Makefile and
games/totk/duo/CMakeLists.txt under games/totk/android/build_release.py, which take the game's title and module from
games/games.toml (tools/games.py). The tree holds the vendored Eden Duo and companion files at the commits above
(games/totk/duo/vendor, with every local change listed in its README.md), the port's shim and host code, the rest of the
app, and its build scripts, which fetch the other third-party sources at pinned commits and name the two prebuilt
inputs (the Turnip driver: its recipe is games/totk/android/turnip; the shader translator: games/totk/tools/totk_xlate).
Where to get it: the section "Source of this APK" at the top of this file, written by the build, names the repository
and the full commit; that commit (browsable at <repository>/tree/<commit>, downloadable as
<repository>/archive/<commit>.tar.gz) is the corresponding source. Anyone who passes this APK on must make that source
available as section 6 of the licence requires.
